Privacy Policy for the “Together” app

2. Overview

Together is an app for shared chores and shopping lists. You can use it without an account; your data then stays on your device. We do not sell personal data.

3. Data on your device

Tasks, shopping lists, reminder times, home name, member names, language and theme are stored locally on your device. They only leave the device if you use the shared home feature (section 4). If a technical error occurs, the error message is also stored locally only, so it can be shown to you at the next start.

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications by your device's operating system. No data is sent to us or third parties for this. You can revoke the notification permission at any time in your device settings.

4. Shared home (optional, Supabase)

If you sign in to share a home with others, we process your email address (sign-in with a one-time code), your display name and the contents of the shared home (tasks including reminder time, shopping list, home name, members). Purpose: providing the shared use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Provider: Supabase Inc.; data is stored in a data centre in London (United Kingdom), covered by an EU adequacy decision. Sign-in codes are sent by email via the email service Resend. Data is deleted when you leave the home or request deletion of your account by email.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads via Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and interactions with ads may be processed to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure reach.

At first start we ask for your consent through Google's consent platform. Personalised ads are only shown with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without consent, non-personalised ads may be shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Home → Privacy settings. More information: How Google uses data.

With a Together Plus subscription no ads are shown.

6. Subscriptions and purchases (Google Play, App Store, RevenueCat)

Subscriptions are purchased and paid through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive payment details. To manage the subscription status we use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA), which processes a random app user ID, purchase receipts, products and times, and device information (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled at any time in the subscription settings of your Google or Apple account.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent with effect for the future. Contact us at the email address above. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 16.

9. Changes

We update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The version published here applies.
